The Fortnightly Draw

Every two weeks, four players are thrown together — two from each squad — and they have that fortnight to do their group 1-2-1 and post it in the chapter's social media group. Do it and every player in the group scores . Miss it and the group pays the fine.

This Round, From The Sheet

The Fines Jar

Fines are money, not points. They never touch the scoreboard — a squad cannot buy its way up the table, and cannot be knocked down it by owing the jar. A fine is owed by the group; the per-squad figures below it are just that fine split by head, so you can see where the jar is coming from.

    Or paste it in

    Select the cells in your sheet, copy, and paste below. Works with one tab at a time. Tell it which tab this is.

    What This Page Expects To Find

    1. Weekly PALMS tabs — any tab with a header row containing First Name and RGI. One tab per meeting week. Visitors, referrals, 1-2-1s, TYFCB and attendance all come from here.
    2. Teams — column A player name, column B squad name. Two squads: the men's and the women's, with any surplus men in the women's squad.
    3. Groups — the fortnightly draw and whether each group delivered. Make the draw on the Group 1-2-1 tab and paste it in.
    4. Training — a Name column plus one column per month.
    5. Adjustments — optional. Only for a points decision made in the room that the PALMS sheet cannot show. See the columns below.

    Groups columns

    Done has three states, not two. yes pays every player in the group; no puts the group in the fines jar; blank means the fortnight is still running and nothing happens either way. Leaving it blank is the honest answer until the two weeks are up.

    Adjustments columns

    Column order does not matter — the engine reads the headings, not the positions. Any heading it does not recognise is ignored, and an old GameLog tab still reads correctly.
