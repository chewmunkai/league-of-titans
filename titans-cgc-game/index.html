Manual Scores

For points the sheet cannot see — a visitor missed off PALMS, a bonus the room gave one person, a correction. Each line is Name | Item | Score: who, what it was for, and the points (a minus number takes points away). It counts for that player and for whichever squad they are on, and shows up line by line in the squad's audit trail.

Paste several at once

One per line, Name | Item | Score. A tab or a comma between them works too, so rows copied straight out of a spreadsheet go in as they are. They count in the month picked above.

The Monthly Draw

Every month the chapter is split into small groups, mixed across both squads, each led by the same mentor every round. They have that month — the 1st to the last day — to do their group 1-2-1 and post it in the chapter's social media group. Do it and every player in the group scores . Miss it and the group pays the fine.

Mentors

Mentors are fixed. Each one leads the same group number every round — the first mentor always leads Group 1 — and only the members around them are reshuffled. Use the arrows to change who leads which group.

The Fines Jar

Fines are money, not points. They never touch the scoreboard — a squad cannot buy its way up the table, and cannot be knocked down it by owing the jar. A fine is owed by the group; the per-squad figures below it are just that fine split by head, so you can see where the jar is coming from.

Roster Board

Drag a name onto the other squad to move them — the whole board recalculates straight away. On a phone, use the small dropdown on each name instead. Your changes wait for you — press Save Changes at the bottom of the screen when you are done.

Add A Player

Someone who has joined the chapter, or who is in the meeting but missing from the sheet. Spell the name exactly as the PALMS tabs spell it, or their activity will not find them.

Rename a squad

Rename a squad here and every player moves with it. To add a third squad, put it in the Teams tab of your sheet — the board follows whatever the sheet says.

    Or paste it in

    Select the cells in your sheet, copy, and paste below. Works with one tab at a time. Tell it which tab this is.

    What This Page Expects To Find

    1. Weekly PALMS tabs — any tab with a header row containing First Name and RGI. One tab per meeting week. Visitors, referrals, 1-2-1s, TYFCB and attendance all come from here.
    2. Teams — column A player name, column B squad name. Two squads: the men's and the women's, with any surplus men in the women's squad.
    3. Groups — the monthly draw and whether each group delivered. Make the draw on the Group 1-2-1 tab and paste it in.
    4. Scoring — optional. Your own scoring lines. If this tab exists it defines the whole list and the defaults in this page step aside.
    5. Training — a Name column plus one column per month.
    6. Adjustments — optional. Only for a points decision made in the room that the PALMS sheet cannot show. See the columns below.
    7. Manual Scores — optional. Name | Item | Score, points added by hand for one player. Made for you the first time one is saved from the Manual Scores tab.

    Scoring columns

    Build the lines on the Scoring tab of this page and hit Copy Scoring For Sheet rather than typing this out — it fills in the keys and spells the sources the way the engine expects.

    Groups columns

    Done has three states, not two. yes pays every player in the group; no puts the group in the fines jar; blank means the month is still running and nothing happens either way. Leaving it blank is the honest answer until the month is up.

    Adjustments columns

    Column order does not matter — the engine reads the headings, not the positions. Any heading it does not recognise is ignored, and an old GameLog tab still reads correctly.
